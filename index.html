Who We Are

Local action. Shared responsibility. Lasting possibility.

Atal Yuva Bahini is committed to community-centred initiatives that bring people together around education, wellness, and practical citizen facilitation.

We believe meaningful development starts by listening to people, respecting local institutions, and creating welcoming spaces where communities can learn, participate, and find guidance.

Our work is guided by Seva O Samarpana — a spirit of service and dedication that places dignity, inclusion, and responsibility at the heart of community engagement.

Featured initiative · Proposed

Community Development

Utkarsha Gopalpur

An integrated community-development concept proposed for Gopalpur Constituency, Ganjam District, Odisha. It brings supplemental education, yoga and wellness, and citizen facilitation into one community-centred model.

  • Designed around locally accessible community spaces, subject to permission.
  • Three complementary roles: Yoga Trainer, Teachers, and Panchayat Relationship Coordinator.
  • Supports public-service navigation without replacing the authority of government departments.
  • Emphasises safeguarding, responsible recordkeeping, and learning from implementation.

Important: The proposal describes an intended programme model. Its proposed scale and activities should not be presented as completed results unless implementation is verified.

Our Approach

Trust is built through responsible action.

We aim to keep community initiatives grounded, transparent, and respectful of the people and institutions involved.

01 / LISTEN

Start with the community

Understand local needs, consult relevant stakeholders, and adapt activities to the context of each community.

02 / ACT

Work within clear boundaries

Seek permission for premises, clarify each role, safeguard children, and complement rather than replace existing services.

03 / LEARN

Record and improve

Keep appropriate records of activities, reflect on challenges, protect personal information, and report only what evidence supports.
