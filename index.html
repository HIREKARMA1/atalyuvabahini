Who We Are

Local action. Shared responsibility. Lasting possibility.

Atal Yuva Bahini is committed to community-centred initiatives that bring people together around education, wellness, and practical citizen facilitation.

We believe meaningful development starts by listening to people, respecting local institutions, and creating welcoming spaces where communities can learn, participate, and find guidance.

Our work is guided by Seva O Samarpana — a spirit of service and dedication that places dignity, inclusion, and responsibility at the heart of community engagement.

Featured initiative · Proposed

Community Development

Utkarsha Gopalpur

An integrated community-development concept proposed for Gopalpur Constituency, Ganjam District, Odisha. It brings supplemental education, yoga and wellness, and citizen facilitation into one community-centred model.

  • Designed around locally accessible community spaces, subject to permission.
  • Three complementary roles: Yoga Trainer, Teachers, and Panchayat Relationship Coordinator.
  • Supports public-service navigation without replacing the authority of government departments.
  • Emphasises safeguarding, responsible recordkeeping, and learning from implementation.

Important: The proposal describes an intended programme model. Its proposed scale and activities should not be presented as completed results unless implementation is verified.

Our Approach

Trust is built through responsible action.

We aim to keep community initiatives grounded, transparent, and respectful of the people and institutions involved.

01 / LISTEN

Start with the community

Understand local needs, consult relevant stakeholders, and adapt activities to the context of each community.

02 / ACT

Work within clear boundaries

Seek permission for premises, clarify each role, safeguard children, and complement rather than replace existing services.

03 / LEARN

Record and improve

Keep appropriate records of activities, reflect on challenges, protect personal information, and report only what evidence supports.

Ways to collaborate

Different partners can contribute in different ways.

The right scope depends on community consultation, partner priorities, organisational capacity, and any applicable requirements. Possible contributions to discuss include:

Programme resources

Learning materials, books, sports or activity supplies, and other resources identified as useful and appropriate for a locally agreed activity.

Skills and knowledge

Voluntary or professional guidance in education, wellness, safeguarding, monitoring, communications, or organisational development.

Planning and learning

Support to clarify intended outcomes, practical indicators, recordkeeping, community feedback, and reflection on what is working.

Financial support

Any funding discussion would require due diligence, an agreed scope and budget, and confirmation of applicable legal and eligibility requirements before commitments are made.

A responsible starting point

Move from conversation to clear expectations.

These steps describe a possible collaboration process. They do not represent a current agreement or guarantee that a proposed activity will proceed.

01 / LISTEN

Understand the context

Discuss community priorities, existing services, local stakeholders, and the partner's areas of interest.

02 / SHAPE

Define a realistic scope

Agree what could be explored, who would be involved, where activities might take place, and what resources may be needed.

03 / AGREE

Document responsibilities

Before starting, clarify roles, permissions, safeguards, budget, timelines, reporting, and any required approvals in writing.

04 / REVIEW

Learn and communicate

Review progress with relevant stakeholders and share only accurate updates supported by appropriate records.

Trust & accountability

Clarity before commitments. Evidence before claims.

Responsible collaboration means being open about what is planned, what is approved, what has taken place, and what has been learned. No partnership or programme result should be described as confirmed until it is verified.

Important: The Utkarsha Gopalpur initiative described above is proposed. Its proposed activities and scale should not be presented as completed results unless implementation is verified.
  • Verify organisational particulars, registrations, eligibility, and due-diligence documents relevant to the proposed collaboration.
  • Agree scope, responsibilities, permissions, budget, timelines, and reporting expectations before activities or funding begin.
  • Set appropriate safeguarding and privacy practices, especially where children or personal information may be involved.
  • Keep proportionate records and communicate activities and outcomes accurately, including limitations and lessons learned.
  • Respect the roles of schools, healthcare providers, government departments, and other local institutions.

Useful to know

Questions partners may have.

Is a programme or funding partnership already confirmed?

This page does not announce a confirmed partnership or funding arrangement. Any future relationship would be communicated only after it has been formally agreed.

Can a partner propose a specific area of support?

Yes. A partner can raise an area of interest for discussion. Any proposal would still need to be assessed for community relevance, feasibility, permissions, safeguards, and applicable requirements.

Does this website accept applications or donations online?

No. This is an informational website only. It has no application, payment, or donation processing system.

How can an organisation start a conversation?

Use Atal Yuva Bahini's approved public communication channels. Contact details and communication processes should be verified before publication.
